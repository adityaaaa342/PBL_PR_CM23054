Viva Voce & Theory Defense Guide

1. What is Newman's Modularity (Q)?

Modularity measures whether edge density inside communities is higher than what would be expected at random. Values > 0.3 indicate strong real-world community clustering structure.

2. Why is Girvan-Newman slower than Louvain?

Girvan-Newman computes edge betweenness across all shortest paths (O(V × E)) after every single edge removal (O(E² × V)). Louvain performs fast greedy local moves in O(V log V) time.

3. What is Conductance (Φ)?

Conductance is the fraction of total edges that cut outside the community boundary into the rest of the network. Lower conductance represents tighter isolation.

4. How is Normalized Mutual Information (NMI) calculated?

NMI measures the shared statistical information between the algorithm's predicted communities and the known ground-truth factions (e.g. 12 football athletic conferences). 1.0 represents a flawless 100% match.
